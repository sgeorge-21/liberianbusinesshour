About The Show

Liberia's Premier Business Radio Program

The Liberian Business Hour is a weekly radio program that airs every Saturday from 7:00 to 7:45 AM on FM in Monrovia, Liberia. For those who miss the live broadcast, the show repeats every Sunday from 5:00 to 5:45 PM on the same station.

Founded with the mission to inform, educate, and empower Liberia's business community, the show covers everything from macroeconomic policy and banking reform to entrepreneurship, agriculture, technology, and trade. Each episode features expert analysis, guest interviews, and actionable insights for business owners, investors, and professionals.

Meet The Host

James T. Worquea III

James T. Worquea III

Host & Executive Producer — The Liberian Business Hour

James T. Worquea III is a Liberian business journalist, radio host, and economic commentator. As the host and executive producer of The Liberian Business Hour on Kear Radio 95.9FM, he has established himself as one of Liberia's most respected voices on business and economic affairs.

His weekly program reaches thousands of business owners, entrepreneurs, investors, and professionals across Liberia and the diaspora, making complex economic issues accessible and actionable for everyday people.
